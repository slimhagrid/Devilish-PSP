Optional XMB art for the PSP menu. The kit ships none (the obvious pictures are the game's own artwork, which is not ours to hand out).
Put your own files here before building and the build picks them up:
    ICON0.png   144 x 80     the icon in the game list
    PIC1.png    480 x 272    the background
    PIC0.png    310 x 180    the preview (optional)
    SND0.AT3                 the background music (optional; needs an AT3 encoder, not part of this kit)
Missing files are skipped.
